## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no trial period. You enter your tools, you get an audit. The only thing we ask for afterward is your email if you want to save the report.

**Q: What tools does SpendLens audit?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. More tools added based on demand.

**Q: How do you know what I should be paying?**
A: Every recommendation is based on official vendor pricing pages, updated weekly. We cite the source for every number. If you think we're wrong, the link is right there.

**Q: What happens to my data?**
A: Your audit data is stored securely and never sold. If you enter your email, Credex may reach out if they can save you money on AI credits. You can opt out any time.

**Q: What is Credex?**
A: Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, and others — from companies that over-bought. They sell the surplus at a discount. SpendLens is their free audit tool. For teams spending >$500/month, they offer a free consultation to see how much you could save via credits.
