# ECONOMICS

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits. Assume:

- Average credit purchase: **$3,000** (one quarter of a mid-size team's AI spend)
- Gross margin on resold credits: **20%** (sourced at ~80 cents on the dollar)
- Gross profit per transaction: **$600**
- Repeat purchase rate: **40%** within 12 months (sticky if discounts hold)
- LTV (2-year, 1.4× purchases average): **$840**

**Converted lead LTV ≈ $840**

## CAC by channel

| Channel | Effort | Est. conversions/mo | CAC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Show HN post | 1 hour | 5–15 | $0 hard cost |
| Cold X DMs | 3 hours/week | 3–8 | $0 hard cost |
| Rands/Latent Space Slack | 1 hour | 2–5 | $0 hard cost |
| Existing Credex customer email | 30 min | 5–20 | $0 hard cost |
| Blog / SEO | 4 hours/post | 2–8/mo (ramps) | $0 hard cost |

All channels are $0 paid budget. At 10–30 qualified leads/month across channels, blended CAC is effectively **labour only** — call it **$50–150** fully-loaded per conversion (founder/intern time).

## Conversion funnel

| Stage | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page → audit completed | 35% | High intent from specific channels |
| Audit completed → email captured | 25% | Value shown first; modal not forced |
| Email captured → consultation booked | 15% | Only for >$500/mo savings cases; ~30% of audits |
| Consultation booked → credit purchase | 40% | Sales-qualified at this point |

**Net: ~0.5% of all visitors become paying customers.**

At 1,000 audits/month → 5 customers/month → $4,200/month gross profit.

---

## What makes this profitable

The tool's CAC is near-zero because:
1. Distribution is content/community (no paid)
2. The product is genuinely useful and shareable
3. The lead quality is pre-qualified — anyone auditing AI spend is already thinking about costs

Break-even: **3 paying customers/month** covers any tooling/infra costs (<$50/month).

## Path to $1M ARR in 18 months

$1M ARR = ~$83K MRR from credit sales.
At $3K average sale and 40% gross margin = **$208K/month in credit volume**.
At 40% repeat rate, need ~100 active customers spending $2K/month each.

**What has to be true:**
1. Tool drives 500+ audits/month by month 6 (content + word of mouth)
2. 15% of audits are >$500/mo savings cases → 75 high-value leads/month
3. 15% consultation → purchase conversion = ~11 new customers/month
4. Churn <5%/month → reach 100 active customers by month 14

This is aggressive but achievable if Show HN and the Credex customer base both land. The biggest risk is consultation-to-purchase conversion — this depends on Credex's sales process, not the tool.

**Single biggest lever:** The Credex existing customer base email. If 200 existing customers each refer one lead, that alone could be 20–30 qualified conversations in week 1.
