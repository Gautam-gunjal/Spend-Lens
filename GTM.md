# GTM

## Exact target user

**Engineering Manager at a 5–25 person Series A startup**, paying for 3+ AI tools across the team, with a combined bill between $500–$5,000/month. They approved each tool individually, have no consolidated view of spend, and are starting to feel pressure to justify the line items to their CEO. They're not a VP of Finance — they're the person who put the Cursor card on the company Amex because it was $20/month and "worth it," and now there are twelve such decisions stacked up.

Secondary user: **Founder/CTO of a pre-Series A startup** who is personally paying for AI tools and suspects they're over-tooled.

## What they Google/scroll before wanting this

- "how much does cursor cost for teams"
- "cursor vs github copilot 2025"
- "chatgpt team vs enterprise pricing"
- "are we overpaying for AI tools"
- Scroll: Hacker News "Ask HN: how do you manage AI tool costs" threads

## Where they hang out

- **Rands Leadership Slack** (engineering managers)
- **Latent Space Discord** (AI-adjacent builders)
- **Hacker News** — Show HN posts reliably pull this audience

## First 100 users in 30 days, $0 budget

1. **Day 1–3: Show HN post.** Title: "Show HN: Free tool that audits your team's AI tool spend and finds savings." HN is the highest-density channel for this exact user. A modest HN run (50–200 upvotes) drives 300–1,000 visitors. Target: 30–60 signups.

2. **Day 3–7: Cold DM 50 engineering managers on X.** Find them via "engineering manager" in bio + tweets about Cursor/Copilot. Message: "I built a free AI spend audit tool after getting a $4,200 Cursor bill — curious if it'd be useful for your team. No signup required: [link]." Target: 5–10 signups.

3. **Day 5–10: Post in Rands Slack #tools channel.** Not promotional — post the audit link with "curious what others are spending on this." Let results speak. Target: 10–15 signups.

4. **Day 7–14: Write one specific, data-rich blog post.** Title: "We audited 50 startup AI tool stacks — here's what they're overspending on." Post to dev.to and personal blog. Cross-post to r/ExperiencedDevs. Target: 15–25 signups.

5. **Day 14–30: Referral loop from shared audit URLs.** Every shared audit link drives cold traffic. Optimise the share page OG image to be screenshot-worthy.

## Unfair distribution channel

Credex already has paying customers — startups that bought AI credits. These people have **proven intent to optimize AI spend**. A direct email to existing Credex customers ("We built a free tool that tells you where you're overspending — here's a link") hits an audience that already trusts Credex and already cares about this problem. No other cold-start tool has this.

## Week-1 traction if this works

- 300+ audit completions
- 80+ email signups (25%+ conversion from audit to lead)
- 3+ organic Hacker News comments linking the tool
- 1 engineering manager tweets a screenshot of their results
